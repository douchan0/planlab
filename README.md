# PlanLab

**A mechanism-switchable computational laboratory for planned-economy coordination.**

[![licence: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
[![python](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.10%2B-blue.svg)](pyproject.toml)
[![tests](https://img.shields.io/badge/tests-371%20passing-brightgreen.svg)](tests/)
[![paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/paper-EN%2035pp%20%C2%B7%20ZH%2031pp-blue.svg)](paper/)

PlanLab is not a simulator of "the planned economy". It is a controlled experiment in
which **the institution is the treatment**: the same Leontief input–output technology,
the same shock realisations, the same information delay and the same accounting are
given to six different coordination mechanisms, and the resulting shortage, welfare,
plan-fulfilment and stability are compared.

It was built to answer one question:

> **How does delay in the information available to a central coordinator — how stale
> and noisy the planner's picture of demand, capacity and inventories is — affect the
> stability and welfare performance of alternative coordination mechanisms in an
> input–output economy; and which mechanism damps, and which amplifies, the resulting
> shortage dynamics?**

Every number in the paper is generated by code here, every hypothesis is adjudicated in
a machine-readable table, and an independent verifier — sharing no code with the engine
— re-derived the mathematics, re-implemented a period from the written specification,
and audited the raw records. Its findings are recorded in [`verification/report.md`](verification/report.md),
including the six that went against the project lead.

## The model in one paragraph

An `n`-sector economy in discrete quarters. Technology is Leontief with a direct-input
matrix `A` taken from a published symmetric I–O table and capacity `x̄ = K/κ`. Each
period: a shock hits capacity or demand; the coordinator observes a **stale, noisy**
picture of the economy (`ℓ` reporting lags plus log-normal measurement error); it forms
output targets, files claims on every good with a priority ladder, and sets next
period's prices. The engine then determines the **short side** — the largest feasible
output vector given capacity, inventories and the claims that outrank production inputs,
which is a small linear programme — and rations the residual by priority.

Everything is audited every period. The identity

```
S₀ + x  =  Σⱼ Z_ij + C + G + I + S_end + spoilage
```

must hold to `1e-8` relative or the run aborts. Spoilage is output that is neither used
nor storable: storage is finite, and unsalable output is recorded rather than silently
accumulated (see [`docs/AMENDMENTS.md`](docs/AMENDMENTS.md) §4 — without that bound, a
compositional mismatch disappears into the inventory series forever).

## The six institutions

| id | institution | historical referent | information it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lp_central` | welfare-maximising central LP; duals reported as shadow prices | Kantorovich, central material balances | the full current state |
| `material_balance` | resource–need balancing with proportional target revision | Gosplan material balances | plan targets + observed stocks |
| `rationing_priority` | administrative quotas, strict ladder | rationing and quota systems | last period's returns |
| `tatonnement` | price board adjusting to observed excess demand | Lange's market socialism (CPB) | prices + observed gaps |
| `market` | within-period Walrasian clearing of the final-use flow | competitive benchmark | current prices |
| `hybrid_dual_track` | plan track at administered prices for share θ, market track for the rest | dual-track reform | both |

The mechanisms differ only in *who decides what*; all receive the same observation
object and face the same shock stream, so differences in outcome are attributable to
the institution. The one non-negotiable identification rule is `MECHANISM_BASE_PARAMS`:
household- and planner-side parameters are held identical across mechanisms, and only
the institutional knob varies.

## What the paper finds

**1. Delay has a closed-form cost.** For a signal corrected in proportion to an
observed gap with effective delay `m` periods, the loop is stable iff

```
g  <  g*(m) = 2 sin[ π / (2(2m+1)) ]  ≈  π/(2m+1)
```

and at the boundary it oscillates with period exactly `2(2m+1)` quarters. Since
`g*(m) ~ π/(2m+1)`, **doubling the delay roughly halves the admissible gain**: the
first period is the expensive one (2 → 1), and what delay bounds is the product
`m·g*(m) ≤ π/2`, not the gain alone. The bound holds only for negative feedback; a
positive loop has no admissible gain at all. Verified to `1e-13` over `m = 0…30` by four
independent routes ([`verification/`](verification/)).

**2. The information rent is paid in output, not in shortage.** With 3% measurement
noise and *no shock at all*, five of six institutions record a shortage rate inside a
1.65-percentage-point band, while output ranges from 0.937 to 0.995 of the base year.
Because household demand is income-elastic, the institution that destroys output also
destroys the demand that would have registered it — **a paper reporting only shortage
rates would conclude, wrongly, that institutions do not matter.**

**3. Taut plans are expensive only when labour is tight.** Revision gain 1.0 with three
balancing rounds costs 18.5% of output at 5% labour slack, 14.6% at 15%, 0.4% at 40%, and
nothing at 100%; either mechanism alone costs nothing at any slack. The channel is a
one-sided projection onto the labour-feasible frontier, and investment — the residual
claimant — capitalises part of the loss permanently.

**4. Downstream dependence propagates; upstream multiplier does not.** Total output loss
rises with a sector's forward linkage `(Ax)_i / x_i` (ρ = +0.51 / +0.57 / +0.41) and is
uncorrelated with the Leontief multiplier (ρ ≈ 0). This is definition-dependent and the
paper reports both: on the textbook row-sum definition the correlation falls to
ρ ≈ +0.31 and is not significant, and under the pre-registered within-block Holm
correction none survives.

**Two honest non-results.** The delay boundary is **rarely reached**: the measured price
loop gain stays below `g*(m)` for most sectors and lags, is crossed only by a
hard-driven loop at long lags, and the pre-registered detection rule fires once, below
the bound — so no crossing is claimed. The dual-track share shows an interior optimum
that is **formally present but economically empty**, smaller than the seed dispersion.
All six pre-registered hypotheses, including these, are adjudicated in
[`paper/tables/hypothesis_adjudication.csv`](paper/tables/hypothesis_adjudication.csv).

## Reproducibility contract

* Every scenario is a self-describing YAML; `planlab run <file>` reproduces it standalone.
* Every run is seeded deterministically from `(scenario_id, seed)`, and mechanisms inside
  a cell share the **same exogenous draws** — common capacity shocks, common noise
  innovations. (The realised demand path is *not* common, because demand is
  income-elastic and income is an outcome of the institution. The paper says so.)
* [`data/metadata/manifest.json`](data/metadata/manifest.json) records the source URL and
  the sha256 of both the raw response and the aggregation map, so a silent upstream
  revision is detectable.
* Every block's `manifest.json` records package versions, git commit, per-cell code
  fingerprints, scenario hashes and seeds.
* **No number in a table is typed by hand.** Tables come from `results/tables/*.csv`, and
  [`scripts/paper_tables.py`](scripts/paper_tables.py) turns them into the exact files the
  manuscript's tokens resolve to.

Independent evidence that this works: [`notebooks/06_reproduce_a_paper_table.ipynb`](notebooks/06_reproduce_a_paper_table.ipynb)
recomputes the 4,080-run B2 table from `runs.parquet` (numeric columns to storage
precision) and regenerates `fig_lag_grid.png` **pixel-identically** (`max|ΔRGB| = 0`);
[`notebooks/03_one_period_walkthrough.ipynb`](notebooks/03_one_period_walkthrough.ipynb)
re-derives a real engine period from the written specification with **every array
bit-identical** (difference `0.0`).

### What is in git, and what is regenerated

Committed: all code, data, scenarios, the aggregate/summary statistics, audit records,
tables, figures, paper sources and PDFs, notebooks with their executed outputs.
Not committed (regenerable with `bash scripts/reproduce.sh`, ~8 min): the per-cell
scenario dumps and the per-period `runs.parquet` / `sector_panel.parquet` panels. That
keeps the repository at ~50 MB instead of ~320 MB without weakening any claim: every
committed number is either a direct statistic or reproducible by one command.

python scripts/build_paper.py --lang zh --input docs/研究报告_过程记录.md --out-stem process_report_zh

**过程性报告** is deliberately not a summary of the paper. It records what the paper
cannot: that the proportional cascade converged to zero and had to be replaced by a
linear programme; that the priority ladder decided whether the economy collapsed; that
inventory accumulated to 17× annual output before storage was bounded; that the
mechanism comparison was initially confounded by differing default parameters; that the
loop gain must be estimated by exogenous displacement rather than regression; and that
**three successive explanations of the central result were wrong** before the
labour-constraint account survived causal isolation.

## Verification

[`verification/run_all.py`](verification/run_all.py) (about a minute) runs six
independent checks:

| check | what it establishes |
|---|---|
| 1 theorem | the delay boundary, by four routes, `m = 0…30` (agreement `2.9e-11`) |
| 2 engine | a from-scratch one-period solver, reproducing the engine bit-for-bit on 192 periods across calm / capacity-binding / tier-binding regimes |
| 3 accounting | 2,344 raw records: identity `1.6e-16`, `Z = A·diag(x)` exact, every inequality and the ladder |
| 4 reproducibility | 80 of 7,364 runs re-executed bit-for-bit; 147,120 per-period values |
| 5 statistics | independently implemented Wilcoxon / Holm / Spearman / permutation; 1,018 published table values re-derived |
| 6 claims | the mechanism claims, by causal isolation |

[`verification/report.md`](verification/report.md) lists **18 findings with dates**, six of
them against the project lead — two errors in the delay-theorem notes, three wrong
mechanism explanations, a corrupted table column, and an undocumented model change.

## Known limitations

All quantities are constant-price **value** units, not physical volumes. The technology
is a single-year Leontief table with no substitution, so a shortage in a good that is 2%
of costs can idle far more than 2% of capacity. There is no money, credit or finance;
households do not optimise intertemporally; firms do not game plan indicators (the
classic "output tonnage" pathology is future work); the economy is closed, so net exports
are absorbed into final demand; and the short-side programme maximises aggregate output,
which under a labour constraint concentrates the cut in labour-intensive sectors — a
property of the maximand, not of the institution.

The three things most worth doing next: give the price loop enough demand elasticity that
the delay boundary becomes genuinely reachable; let firms misreport their input
requirements, which activates Kornai's soft budget constraint on the information side; and
back-test the labour-slack conditional against real material-balance records.

## 中文摘要

**PlanLab 是一个「制度可替换」的计划经济协调机制计算实验平台。**

它并不试图复刻某个历史国家的全部制度，而是把**制度写成一个可替换的模块**：同一套列
昂惕夫投入产出技术、同一组外生冲击、同样的信息延迟与同样的会计标准，分给六种协调机制
（中央线性规划／影子价格、物资平衡、行政配额、Lange 式价格试错、竞争市场、计划—市场
双轨），比较其短缺、福利、计划完成率与稳定性。

**四项主要结果：**

1. **延迟有闭式代价**：临界增益 `g*(m) = 2sin[π/(2(2m+1))] ≈ π/(2m+1)`，边界处振荡周期
   恰为 `2(2m+1)` 个季度；被约束的是增益与时滞之积（≤ π/2），而非增益本身。
2. **信息租金以产出支付，不以短缺支付**：在完全没有冲击、只有 3% 测量噪声时，五个机制
   的短缺率落在 1.65 个百分点内，而产出比为 0.937–0.995。只报短缺率会错误地得出「制度
   无关紧要」。
3. **紧绷的计划只在劳动力紧张时昂贵**：劳动供给因子 1.05 时损失 18.5%，1.15 时 14.6%，
   1.40 时 0.4%，2.00 时为零；单独的目标修订或单独的平衡都不产生损失。
4. **下游依赖传播，上游乘数不传播**：总产出损失随前向关联上升（ρ = +0.51/+0.57/+0.41），
   与 Leontief 乘数无关（ρ ≈ 0）——但改用教科书定义则不显著，论文两种都报。

**两个诚实的非结果**：延迟边界**极少被真正触及**（预注册判据只在 18 个单元中触发 1 次，
且触发点位于边界之下，故不宣称跨越）；双轨制的内部最优**形式存在但经济上空洞**。

六个预注册假设的裁决见 [`paper/tables/hypothesis_adjudication.csv`](paper/tables/hypothesis_adjudication.csv)；
六次实质返工、三次错误的机制解释与独立验证抓出的 18 条问题，见
[`paper/process_report_zh.pdf`](paper/process_report_zh.pdf)（过程性报告）与
[`verification/report.md`](verification/report.md)。
